### slit-lamp.png — final prompt

Create a refined editorial photograph for the hero of a real ophthalmic surgeon's personal website, vertical portrait 4:5 composition. The subject must be immediately recognizable as ophthalmology: a complete modern slit-lamp biomicroscope on its exam table, with paired black binocular eyepieces, white microscope housing, vertical illumination tower, clear chin rest and forehead rest, adjustment joystick and focusing knobs visible. A slight three-quarter view at eye level. The instrument fills most of the frame, all major functional parts are coherent and physically plausible. A quiet neutral clinic background softly out of focus, warm chalk walls and a subtle gray-sage accent, natural window light, soft graphite shadows. Realistic matte white and black medical materials. Precise, calm, human-scale, understated photographic art direction matching an off-white and muted sage minimalist website. No humans, no logos, no writing, no diagrams, no floating objects, no giant iris, no decorative glass blobs, no sci-fi. This is an illustrative generic instrument photograph, not a representation of a named doctor's actual clinic.

### eye-examination.png — final prompt

Create a professional editorial medical photograph for the 'How the consultation works' section of a minimalist ophthalmic surgeon website, horizontal 4:3 composition. A clearly recognizable non-invasive eye examination at a slit-lamp. Adult female patient in natural side profile at right with chin on the chin rest and forehead gently at the support; the white slit-lamp microscope with black binocular eyepieces and narrow illumination tower occupy the center. At left only the shoulder and forearm of an anonymous clinician in a plain pale gray medical uniform; clinician face fully outside the image. Clinician hand naturally rests on the instrument joystick, without touching the patient's eye. No needles or surgical tools. Calm ordinary diagnostic consultation, not an operating room. Physically realistic anatomy and coherent instrument mechanics. Soft natural daylight, warm off-white surroundings, muted gray-sage background, subtle graphite details, editorial photographic realism with restrained contrast. No text, logos, watermarks, floating lenses, diagrams, artificial eye glow or giant iris. Generic illustrative scene, not a photograph of a specific real doctor or clinic.
